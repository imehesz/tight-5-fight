Tight 5 FIGHT! — sound tester

This page helps us find why the game restarts itself on iPhone. We know sound is involved, but not whether it is one bad sound, one type of sound, or simply too many playing at once. You do not need to know anything technical.

  1. Pick a test below (or tick sounds by hand further down).
  2. Tap the big PLAY button.
  3. Fight for at least 5 minutes — the problem shows up during busy fighting, not while walking around.
  4. Come back and tell us: did it restart, and after how long?

Tests — work down this list

Do them in order. Each one tells us something different, so please report the result even when nothing goes wrong.

Test ONE sound at a time

iPhones currently play no sound effects at all — music only. Each link below puts back exactly one sound and keeps the rest silent, so you can work through them on a borrowed iPhone without rebuilding anything. Play a few minutes on each.

Tick a sound to turn it OFF. The link at the bottom updates as you go.
